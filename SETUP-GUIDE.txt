LUXURY DIGITAL CARD BUILDER — ETSY PRODUCT PROTOTYPE

HOW THE BUYER USES IT
1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Fill in name, title, phone, email, website and social links.
3. Upload a photo or logo if desired.
4. Pick accent/background colors.
5. Click Download Card.
6. Upload the downloaded HTML file to a web host.
7. Share the live URL or QR code.
